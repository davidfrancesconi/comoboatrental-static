Cernobbio & Villa d'Este, 2 hours.

Two hours is the sweet spot for guests who want more than a teaser without committing to a half-day. From the Como pontoon we head along the celebrity coastline — Villa Olmo, Cernobbio with Villa d'Este's century-old plane trees on the water, then up to Villa Oleandra (George Clooney's place in Laglio) and Villa Passalacqua (Moltrasio).

We slow down for photos and answer questions about who lives where, what was filmed where, and why this stretch of shoreline has been the Italian aristocracy's holiday escape for 500 years. Free time to swim or just sit with a glass of prosecco.

The classic 2-hour route, with optional extension by 30 minutes if everyone's having fun and we have the slot.

Frequently asked questions

Can we swim during a 2-hour tour?

Yes — we pause at a quiet cove between Cernobbio and Moltrasio. 15 minutes in the water doesn't slow us down.

What if the weather turns?

We decide morning-of based on the actual lake. If we cancel, full refund. If we shorten, pro-rata refund.

Is this enough to see Villa del Balbianello?

No — Balbianello is 60 minutes one-way from Como. You need the 3-hour or 4-hour tour for that. This one stays in the first basin.
